# Play Store Setup Guide

## Step 1: Create Developer Account
1. Go to https://play.google.com/console
2. Pay $25 one-time fee
3. Complete developer profile

## Step 2: Create App
1. Click "Create app"
2. App name: SubHunt
3. Default language: English
4. App or game: App
5. Free: Yes
6. Accept declarations → Continue

## Step 3: Store Listing
- **Short description** (80 chars):
  Track every subscription. Never miss a bill. Save hundreds per year.

- **Full description** (4000 chars):
  SubHunt helps you track all your subscriptions in one place and take control of your spending.

  The average household spends $273/month on subscriptions but thinks it's only $86. SubHunt shows you exactly where your money goes.

  FEATURES:
  • Add subscriptions in seconds
  • See monthly and yearly spending at a glance
  • Get reminders before bills are due
  • Track by category (Entertainment, Finance, Health, etc.)
  • Export your data to CSV
  • 12 beautiful categories with colors
  • Dark mode with Material You

  PRIVACY FIRST:
  • No bank linking required
  • No ads, ever
  • Your data stays on your device
  • No tracking, no selling data

  FREE TIER:
  • Track up to 3 subscriptions
  • Basic dashboard
  • Bill reminders

  PRO ($4.99/month or $29.99/year):
  • Unlimited subscriptions
  • Detailed analytics
  • CSV export
  • Category breakdown
  • Priority support

## Step 5: Content Rating
- Complete IARC questionnaire
- Expected: Everyone (no sensitive content)

## Step 6: Data Safety
- Data collected: None (local-only app)
- Data shared: None
- Data encrypted: N/A (no network transmission)
- Deletion: User can delete all data via Settings

## Step 7: Pricing
- Countries: All countries
- Price: Free
- In-app purchases configured in Play Console

## Step 8: Release
1. Upload signed AAB (app-bundle)
2. Staged rollout: 10% → 50% → 100%
3. Submit for review (usually 1-3 days)
