Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Introduction

SubHunt ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application SubHunt (the "App").

Data Collection

SubHunt does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

All subscription data you enter into the App is stored exclusively on your device. We do not have access to your data, and we cannot see or retrieve it.

What We Don't Collect

  • Personal identification information
  • Financial data or bank account information
  • Subscription details you enter
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Device identifiers
  • Location data

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Android's Room database. No data is synced to any cloud service or external server.

Third-Party Services

SubHunt uses the following third-party services:

  • RevenueCat — Handles subscription purchases and license validation. RevenueCat's privacy policy applies to payment processing.
  • Google AdMob — Not used. SubHunt contains no advertisements.

Children's Privacy

SubHunt does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The App is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us
