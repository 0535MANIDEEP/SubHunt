Detailed Breakdown

🏆 SubHunt — Best for Privacy-Conscious Users

Pros: 100% private (no bank linking, no accounts, no data collection), full offline support, open source, lifetime pricing option, Health Score for subscription habits, detailed spending insights.

Cons: Android only, manual entry required, smaller user base.

Best for: People who want subscription visibility without sharing financial data. Privacy-first users. Android users who prefer one-time payments.

🏆 Rocket Money — Best for Automatic Tracking

Pros: Automatic bank import, bill negotiation service, cross-platform (Android, iOS, Web), large user base.

Cons: Requires bank linking (privacy concern), bill negotiation takes 25-40% of savings, subscription required for full features, no lifetime option.

Best for: Users who want automatic tracking and don't mind sharing bank data. People who want bill negotiation.

🏆 Bobby — Best for iOS Simplicity

Pros: Clean iOS-native design, simple interface, lifetime option available.

Cons: iOS only, limited insights, no health score, no export options.

Best for: iOS users who want a simple, no-frills subscription tracker.

FAQ

Is SubHunt better than Rocket Money?

It depends on your priorities. SubHunt is better for privacy (no bank linking, all data local). Rocket Money is better for automation (automatic bank import, bill negotiation). If privacy matters to you, SubHunt is the better choice. If convenience matters more, Rocket Money is better.

Can SubHunt automatically import subscriptions?

No. SubHunt requires manual entry. This is by design — it keeps your data 100% private. If you want automatic import, you'll need to link your bank, which SubHunt never does.

Is SubHunt really free?

Yes. Track up to 3 subscriptions free forever. No credit card required. Pro plans ($4.99/mo or $29.99/yr) unlock unlimited subscriptions and premium features.

Which app saves the most money?

Rocket Money's bill negotiation can save money on existing bills, but they take 25-40% of the savings. SubHunt's insights help you identify and cancel unused subscriptions, which typically saves $150-400/year without any service fees.

Is my data safe with SubHunt?

Yes. SubHunt stores all data locally on your device. No data is sent to any server. We have no access to your data. This is verified by our open-source privacy policy.
